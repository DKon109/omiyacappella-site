A change to who we are looking for Decided by a poll within the group

Changed from "at least two years in a student or adult a cappella circle" to "at least two years in a student a cappella circle (past or present)". When applicants came from adult circles it was often impossible to tell how much singing they had actually done, which made the decision difficult.
