Omiya Acappella Platform

Sing a cappella, any time, in Omiya

Looking for an a cappella group around Omiya in Saitama? OMIYAcappella does not put you in a fixed band. You sing the song you want, when you want to — in one-day or short-term project bands that anyone can start. Open to experienced singers.
